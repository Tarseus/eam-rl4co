## Lessons and constraints

- The user-authorized CVRP50 diagnostic reproduced USW exactly at `10.437088506984711` and measured fixed epoch778 at `10.441343907976151`, but SymNCO produced `10.587122968816757` instead of locked `10.43764`. The rollout recipe is not reproduced, so its paired statistics are invalid for paper claims. Raw audit data is under `experiments/cvrp50_asw_after_usw/results/diagnostic_rollout_mismatch/`.
- The final preregistered FFSP50 LR3e-6 screen completed and failed its source-validation gate. No further FFSP50 LR bracket is scheduled; the old test remains prohibited and the untouched n2000 set remains unread.

- Keep the original two-sided Wilcoxon plus global Holm family; do not switch to a more favorable test post hoc.
- Select checkpoints only by validation data, then evaluate once on the locked paper instances.
- An ASW continuation initialized from USW must be reported as `ASW-after-USW`, not silently substituted for the original single-stage ASW method.
- Improving the mean direction was insufficient for both locked USW candidates; neither paper checkpoint registry entry should be replaced.
