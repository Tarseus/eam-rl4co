# Official SLIM Baseline Recovery Findings

## Current understanding

- Historical `sll_loss` results measure a custom listwise objective, not published SLIM.
- The main BOPO/PO comparisons remain valid, but every SLL/SLIM-labelled row must be replaced or relabelled.
- Unit tests pass locally and on g51 (19/19). All eight primary one-batch smokes now pass with finite losses: TSP50 0.866, TSP100 1.287, CVRP50 0.532, CVRP100 0.864, FFSP50 1.355, FFSP100 1.805, JSSP10x10 2.151, and JSSP15x15 2.501.
- g51's current shared `jssp_bopo/train` contains only 10x10 instances. JSSP15 therefore uses the already-existing shape-isolated 96-instance `jssp_scale_probe/15x15/train` split; its validation stream is the 100 filtered 15x15 instances from the common validation directory. This is explicit in the config and prevents silent mixed-shape loading.
- An unintended inherited `test=true` caused the routing/FFSP smoke only to touch fixed test data. Those values are discarded and no choice was made from them; every full configuration is now `test=false` until a validation-selected checkpoint is locked.
- Full validation-only training is now live concurrently for all eight primary paper problems: JSSP10/15 on g51, the three size-50 problems on g52, and the three size-100 problems on g53. No primary problem remains queued and no full-run test data have been read.
- The first full gates are healthy: JSSP10 reached epoch1 validation makespan 887.274 with train loss 1.361629, while JSSP15 reached epoch0 validation makespan 1434.110 with train loss 2.367374. Both continued beyond those gates and both wrapper logs have zero error-pattern matches.
- Cross-host deployment uses an isolated tracked-source snapshot at revision `ac5d7e4c1` with archive SHA256 `c9a92e...f0b6`, while explicitly linking each runtime to its host's existing data directory. The first distributed setup attempt is excluded because its six processes shared a runtime-local default-data generation directory; the corrected six jobs have separate result roots, active GPU allocations, and zero error-pattern matches.
- The first corrected distributed routing gates are finite and well beyond startup: TSP50/CVRP50/TSP100/CVRP100 reached steps 799/549/349/249 with SLIM losses 0.148534/0.204038/0.275430/0.404543. FFSP50/100 are still within their first accumulated epoch but remain GPU-active and error-free.
- JSSP10/15 training is complete and validation-locked without test access. JSSP10 selects epoch19 at makespan 866.048401; JSSP15 selects epoch14 at 1332.069946 rather than the worse terminal epoch19. Their legacy queue wrappers are gone, leaving six active distributed trainers, two completed trainers, and zero failures.
- The two locked JSSP checkpoints have now been tested once under a checkpoint-matched common-random-number protocol (100 fixed generated instances per shape, B128, greedy0, no augmentation). Their test means are 872.289996 (6.055210% gap) for JSSP10 and 1346.899988 (11.704688% gap) for JSSP15; these are test results and must not be confused with the validation-selection means 866.048401 and 1332.069946.
- Official SLIM does not challenge the strongest preference baselines on these JSSP tests. On JSSP10 it trails BOPO/loss-only/weighting by 17.82/19.16/20.73 makespan; on JSSP15 it trails them by 43.73/38.40/49.36. Every one of these six losses is significant under two-sided paired Wilcoxon with Holm correction over the 12 SLIM comparisons, and every bootstrap 95% CI is wholly above zero.
- SLIM significantly improves over RL on JSSP10 (18.51 lower; 95% bootstrap CI 13.84 to 23.23 lower; `p_Holm=4.48e-10`) but not on JSSP15 (3.97 lower; CI spans -3.25 to 11.40 lower; `p_Holm=0.3686`). It decisively beats PO and the historical custom-listwise `sll` checkpoint on both shapes. This supports relabelling the old `sll` rows, but not claiming that official SLIM beats BOPO, USW, or ASW.
- Direct comparison with BOPO Table 1 changes the interpretation of these two checkpoints. On LA10x10, the locked JSSP10 model is only 0.93/1.08 gap points worse than paper SLIM_MGL at B'=128/512. On JSSP15, however, it is 4.05/4.47 points worse on TA15x15 and 3.61/2.97 points worse on LA15x15. This is too large to call a paper-level reproduction.
- The discrepancy has a concrete protocol cause: both current JSSP SLIM models saw only 96 training instances of one shape, while BOPO Table 1's training protocol uses 30000 instances across six shapes (5000 per shape). The current JSSP rows must therefore be labelled small-data SLIM-on-MGL supplements until a paper-protocol multishape reproduction or an official checkpoint is available. The paper gives aggregate gaps but no aligned per-instance predictions, so significance against its SLIM/SLIM_MGL rows cannot be calculated from published evidence.
